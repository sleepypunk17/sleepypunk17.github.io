My Passion

I'm an avid fan of the Wings of Fire series. The intricate world-building, compelling characters, and epic dragon adventures captivate me. Whether it's following Seawing intrigue, Sandwing politics, or Skywing drama, I love immersing myself in Pyrrhia's complex ecosystems and conflicts. The series combines fantasy, strategy, and emotional depth that keeps me coming back for more.

Dragon Tribes I Love

🌊 Seawings

Mysterious underwater dragons with intricate political systems and hidden secrets beneath the waves.

🔥 Skywings

Proud aerial warriors with their rigid hierarchy and dramatic mountain citadels.

🏜️ Sandwings

Desert-dwelling dragons torn by political conflict and ancient prophecies.

🌲 Rainwings

Colorful rainforest dragons with amazing camouflage and unique culture.

❄️ Icewings

Cold and strategic dragons from the frozen lands with deep family bonds.

🌙 Nightwings

Mysterious and powerful dragons with extraordinary abilities and secrets.

🌱 Mudwings

Sturdy mud-dwelling dragons with strong family loyalty and hard bodies.

🍃 Leafwings

Forest dragons with plant-based abilities and a tragic history of survival and rebirth.

🐞 Beetlewings

Armored insect-like dragons with shimmering exoskeletons and hive-like communities.

🪡 Silkwings

Gentle silk-producing dragons known for their artistic talents and graceful wings.

💨 Wildwings

Dragons that mixed with so many different tribes so they don't look like any single tribe.

⚔️ Sharpwings

Dragons that were enchanted 3000 years ago to guard an cursed island.

🐝 Hivewings

Hive-organized dragons with collective intelligence and powerful tribal connections.
